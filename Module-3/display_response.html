Field & Record Descriptions

  • Full Name: Applicant's full name (text).
  • Email: Contact email (text/email).
  • Phone: Contact phone number (text/tel).
  • Position: Role the applicant is applying for (select).
  • Start Date: Date the applicant is available to start (date).
  • Salary: Desired annual salary in USD (numeric).
  • Education: Highest education level (radio single choice).
  • Years of Experience: Numeric field; used to compute Experience Category.
  • Skills: Multi-select checkboxes; displayed as CSV list.
  • Cover Letter: Free-text remarks (textarea).
